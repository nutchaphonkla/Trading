V37 MT5 FALLBACK

1. Open OneMonth_Feed_Bridge.mq5 in MetaEditor.
2. Compile (F7).
3. Attach EA to XAUUSD/XAUUSDm.
4. In Inputs set InpWebhookToken to your existing Cloudflare WEBHOOK_TOKEN.
5. Keep InpBackfillOnStart=true and InpBackfillBars=5000 initially.
6. Tools > Options > Experts: Allow WebRequest for
   https://onemonth-tv-feed.nutchaphonsit.workers.dev

This EA is DATA ONLY. It does not place/close/modify trades.
The MT5 fallback only becomes ACTIVE when Twelve Data is unavailable/stale.
